How SkillSwap Works

Simple steps to start exchanging skills

1. Create Your Profile

Sign up and list the skills you have to offer and the skills you want to learn.

2. Find a Match

Browse through skills or use our search to find someone with complementary skills.

3. Send a Swap Request

Connect with other users and propose a skill exchange.

4. Learn & Teach

Schedule sessions and start exchanging knowledge with your skill partner.
